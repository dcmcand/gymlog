GymLog has a new app ID (io.github.dcmcand.gymlog) so it can be published on F-Droid and Google Play. It installs as a separate app: in your old GymLog (1.5 or later) use Settings > Export data, then here use Settings > Import data, check your history, and uninstall the old app. Move before your next workout: once 2.0 is installed, the Pebble watch app talks only to it.
